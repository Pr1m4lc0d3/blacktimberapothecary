Privacy

What we collect

Short version: a visit counter you can switch off, and whatever you type into the inquiry form. There's no account to make, no newsletter running on this domain, and nothing sold to anyone.

Visit counting

We run Google Analytics 4 to see which pages people read. It's off until you accept the cookie notice. Decline and the tag never loads a cookie or sends a hit, and the site works the same. Your choice is stored in your own browser under bta-consent, so clearing site data brings the notice back.

If you accept, Google receives your truncated IP address, the pages you viewed, your approximate city, and your device and browser type. It doesn't receive your name or email, because this site never asks for them outside the form below.

The inquiry form

Wholesale and order inquiries go through FormSubmit, which relays the message to our inbox. FormSubmit sees what you typed and your IP address while it passes the message along. We keep the email until the conversation is finished, then delete it.

Gear orders

Mugs and glassware are fulfilled by Fourthwall on their own storefront. Once you leave this site for theirs, their privacy policy and their payment processor apply, not ours. We never see your card details anywhere.

Hosting

The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs requests for security and abuse handling. There's no database here and no server-side code, so there's nowhere for us to store anything about you even if we wanted to.

What we don't do

No advertising pixels. No Facebook or Meta tracking, although we do link to our page. No profiling, no data broker, no email list on this domain. We don't knowingly collect anything from children.

Getting your data removed

Send a request through the form on the wholesale page and say what you want deleted. We'll delete the email thread and confirm. For the analytics side, declining the cookie notice or clearing your browser data removes your identifiers outright. If you're in the UK or the EU, you can also complain to your national data protection authority.
